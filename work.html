Step One — The Research

We learn the trade before we build for it.

Before design started we learned how saunas are sold and installed in Canada: the electrical rules, the heater maths, when a permit is triggered, and how the season moves demand.

Electrical is the hidden blocker

The panel is where a lot of projects quietly die. The site raises it early, not at quote stage.

Heater sizing drives the whole spec

Volume, glass and bench layout decide the heater, which decides the price. The intake asks up front.

Permits vary by municipality

The honest answer is "it depends where you are." The agent says that, then routes to a human.

Buyers research for months

The follow-up is built for a long window, so a spring enquiry is still warm in autumn.

Step Two — The Build

Then we build around what we found.

Everything below exists because of something in the research above.

The Site
Built from scratch around their own products and photography, each range with its own page. No template, nothing borrowed.
The Agent
Trained on their specs, not the open internet. It talks cedar, heaters and lead times the way the owner would, and holds firm lines: no price, no permit promise, no health claim.
The Intake
Property type, dimensions and postal code captured before the enquiry is sent. Enquiries arrive already qualified.
The Backend
Enquiries land in the CRM, consults on the calendar, and follow-up runs on its own.
Deliberately Absent
No prices on the page. Every job is specified differently, and a number without a spec later has to move.

The research doesn't show up on the page. It shows up in the questions the site already knows how to answer.
